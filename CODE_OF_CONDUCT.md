# Code of conduct

## Our standards

We commit to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful, give actionable feedback, assume good intent while addressing harmful impact, and keep technical disagreement focused on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, sexualized attention, threats, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Responsible use of AI

AI-assisted contributions are welcome when they are reviewed and owned by the contributor. Do not use AI to impersonate another person, fabricate evidence or attribution, disclose confidential or personal information, overwhelm maintainers with unreviewed output, or evade licensing and authorship responsibilities. Clearly disclose material AI assistance when it affects provenance, security, licensing, or the reviewability of a contribution.

Generated suggestions never excuse harmful conduct or transfer responsibility away from the person submitting them.

## Scope and enforcement

These expectations apply in repository discussions, reviews, issues, and other spaces where someone represents this project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact options on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/RamonDevPrivate). Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be reviewed with discretion and without retaliation.
